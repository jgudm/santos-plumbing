FAQ

Common questions

What should I do first if a pipe is leaking?

Turn off the main water valve if you can, move anything that could get wet, then call us.

Do you work with homes and businesses?

Yes. We handle plumbing for houses, condos, shops and offices in Cebu City.

How do I get a price?

Call or send a WhatsApp message with the problem and a photo if you can. We’ll tell you what the job needs.

Why does my drain keep blocking?

Grease, hair and soap often build up inside the pipe. We clear the blockage and tell you what’s causing it.
